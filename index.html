About Me

I am a graduate researcher at Carnegie Mellon University Africa working at the intersection of deep learning and medical imaging. My research focuses on making diagnostic AI accurate, efficient, and deployable in low-resource clinical settings.

My work spans brain tumor classification, diabetic retinopathy screening, breast cancer risk triage, and MRI reconstruction for hospitals where high-field scanners and specialist care are scarce. I believe that building solutions for low resource setting is building a solution that works anywhere

Before graduate school, I spent years building production systems with Django and FastAPI — engineering experience that now informs how I design AI systems that move from notebooks to real-world deployment.

Under Review Brain Tumor Classification
FPN-Mamba: A Feature Pyramid Network with Bidirectional State Space Models for Medical MRI Image Classification
Adetoro Damilola Simbiat, Samuel Akinwumi Adeniji · Carnegie Mellon University
Integrates a Feature Pyramid Network with Bidirectional Mamba SSMs for multi-scale MRI classification. A LocalityMixing block at each pyramid level fuses depthwise local convolution with global Mamba context via a learned gate, preserving fine spatial structure in linear time O(L). Applied to medulloblastoma screening, surpassing InceptentionNet on four of five metrics.

Resource-Efficient Two-Stage Diabetic Retinopathy and Macular Edema Screening via Cascade Classification and Offline Mobile Deployment
Adetoro Simbiat Damilola, Lobago Moan, Godwill Nkwain Nyong, Boniface Godwin · Carnegie Mellon University Africa
A two-stage cascade framework for joint DR severity grading and DME detection designed for CPU-only deployment in low-resource clinics. Module 1 grades DR severity (MobileNetV2, F1=0.726); its prediction serves as a clinically motivated auxiliary input to Module 2, a PCA-compressed dual-backbone soft-voting ensemble. Packaged as DR-tictation, an offline mobile app delivering outputs in under one second via a clip-on smartphone fundus lens.

03
Ongoing Medical VLM
Cross-modal JEPA for Medical Image-Text Grounding: Learning Spatially and Temporally Structured Representations for Clinical Decision Support
Damilola Simbiat Adetoro · Carnegie Mellon University Africa
A language-conditioned JEPA framework where the predictor is guided by paired radiology reports rather than predicting hidden patches blindly, forcing the model to learn what clinical descriptions refer to spatially in the image. Pretraining on MIMIC-CXR (227k chest X-ray and report pairs), evaluated on CheXpert, LIDC-IDRI, BraTS, and PathVQA. Targets better spatial grounding and interpretability versus CLIP-based medical VLMs like BioViL and MedCLIP.

Breast Cancer Risk Triage System
Self-screening support app returning a colour-coded risk band (GREEN / YELLOW / RED) with plain-language guidance. Combines a Gradient Boosting model trained on real UCI Wisconsin FNA biopsy data with 8 clinical safety flags grounded in ACS 2023, NICE NG101, and BI-RADS 5th Edition. Flags run independently and can only escalate a result. Deployed via FastAPI and Docker with a full step-by-step UI.
