FPN-Mamba: A Feature Pyramid Network with Bidirectional State Space Models for Medical MRI Image Classification
Simbiat Damilola Adetoro, Samuel Akinwumi Adeniji · Carnegie Mellon University
Integrates a Feature Pyramid Network with Bidirectional Mamba State Space Models for multi-scale MRI classification. A LocalityMixing block at each pyramid level fuses local depthwise convolution with global Mamba context via a learned gate — preserving fine spatial structure while operating in linear time O(L) instead of the quadratic O(L²) cost of self-attention. Applied to medulloblastoma screening (the most common malignant brain tumor in children), the model surpasses the InceptentionNet baseline on four of five metrics.

Resource-Efficient Two-Stage Diabetic Retinopathy and Macular Edema Screening via Cascade Classification and Offline Mobile Deployment
Adetoro Simbiat Damilola, Lobago Moan, Godwill Nkwain Nyong, Boniface Godwin · Carnegie Mellon University Africa
A two-stage cascade framework for joint DR severity grading and DME detection, designed for CPU-only deployment in low-resource clinics. Module 1 performs five-class DR grading (MobileNetV2, F1=0.726); its prediction serves as a clinically motivated auxiliary input to Module 2, a PCA-compressed dual-backbone soft-voting ensemble for binary DME detection. The full pipeline is packaged as DR-tictation — an offline mobile app that delivers simultaneous DR and DME outputs in under one second via a clip-on smartphone fundus lens, with no GPU or internet required.

Uncertainty-Aware Reconstruction for Low-Field MRI Scans
Simbiat Damilola Adetoro et al. · Carnegie Mellon University Africa
Ongoing research developing probabilistic deep learning models for enhancing image quality in low-field MRI systems — the type of scanner available in rural African hospitals. Incorporates uncertainty estimation to communicate reconstruction confidence to clinicians, improving reliability and trustworthiness of AI-enhanced scans in settings where high-field MRI is inaccessible.
